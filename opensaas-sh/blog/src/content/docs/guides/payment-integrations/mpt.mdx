First, make sure you've defined your payment processor in `src/payment/paymentProcessor.ts` and `src/payment/paymentProcessorClient.ts`, as described in the [important first steps](/guides/payment-integrations/).

[MPT](https://mpt.hr) lets your users pay with a SEPA bank transfer. They scan a QR code (EPC or Croatian HUB3) in their banking app, and the euros arrive at your [Safe](https://safe.global) as EURe, an e-money token issued by [Monerium](https://monerium.com), a regulated e-money institution. MPT tells your app about the payment with a signed webhook.

:::caution[One-time payments only]
A bank transfer is a one-time push payment, so MPT can't renew a subscription. Use it for plans with a `credits` effect (e.g. `Credits10`). Checking out a subscription plan (`Hobby`, `Pro`) throws an error.
:::

### Get sandbox keys

You don't need an account to start. One call gives you sandbox keys:

```sh
curl -s -X POST https://mpt.domovina.ai/api/sandbox/tenants \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"name":"My SaaS (dev)"}'
```

Copy the values from the response into your `.env.server` file:

```ini title=".env.server"
MPT_API_URL=https://mpt.domovina.ai
MPT_SECRET_KEY=<secret_key>
MPT_WEBHOOK_SECRET=<webhook_secret>
MPT_TARGET_ADDRESS=<demo_target_address>
```

The keys are shown only once. In the sandbox no real money moves: you simulate the payment with a button on the MPT checkout page.

If you want webhooks during development, add `"webhook_url":"https://<your-tunnel>/payments-webhook"` to the call above. You don't need them, though (see [Webhooks](#webhooks)).

### Production

For production you need your own [Monerium](https://monerium.com) account and an MPT tenant: contact MPT through [mpt.hr](https://mpt.hr). You then swap in the production `MPT_SECRET_KEY`, `MPT_WEBHOOK_SECRET` and your Safe as `MPT_TARGET_ADDRESS`. Nothing else changes. A sandbox key never works against a production tenant, and the other way around.

### See it with real Monerium

The simulated sandbox skips the bank and the blockchain. To see the full path, there is a public MPT tenant on Monerium's sandbox (`opensaas-sandbox`): a test SEPA transfer is issued as EURe by Monerium and minted on Gnosis Chiado straight into the project's Safe. Its transactions are public: [`opensaas-sandbox-demo` Safe on Blockscout](https://gnosis-chiado.blockscout.com/address/0xAbC300d2D6c838c0bf5FC5DA80De2693e092ef8e).

Your own production setup has the same shape: your Monerium account, your IBAN, your Safe.

### Set your plan prices

MPT has no product catalog: a payment is just an amount. So for MPT, each `PAYMENTS_*_PLAN_ID` env var holds the plan's **price in EUR**:

```ini title=".env.server"
PAYMENTS_CREDITS_10_PLAN_ID=9.99
# Subscriptions are not supported by MPT, but the env vars are still required.
PAYMENTS_HOBBY_SUBSCRIPTION_PLAN_ID=0
PAYMENTS_PRO_SUBSCRIPTION_PLAN_ID=0
```

### Webhooks

MPT sends events to your server's `/payments-webhook` endpoint and signs them with the [Standard Webhooks](https://www.standardwebhooks.com/) scheme. Open SaaS verifies the signature and handles each event once:

| Event | What Open SaaS does |
|---|---|
| `intent.paid` | Adds the plan's credits to the user. |
| `payment.late` | Same as `intent.paid` if the full amount arrived after the payment window closed; otherwise nothing. |
| `payment.received` | Nothing yet. Monerium holds the funds and `intent.paid` follows, usually within seconds. |
| `payment.rejected`, `payment.underpaid` | Logs a warning and grants nothing. |

You don't need a webhook to start developing. After checkout, MPT sends the buyer back to `/checkout?session_id=<sid>`, and the page asks your server to confirm the payment with MPT directly (`confirmCheckoutSession`). The purchase is applied under the same event id the webhook would use, so it is never applied twice.

In production, register `https://<your-server-url>/payments-webhook` with MPT anyway: a buyer can close the tab before a bank transfer arrives, and only the webhook reaches your app then.

### Test a payment

1. Start your app with `wasp start` and log in.
2. Go to the pricing page and buy the credits plan. You are redirected to the MPT checkout page.
3. Click the button that simulates a successful payment. Other buttons simulate a late, short or rejected payment.
4. Back in your app, the checkout page waits for the payment and then shows it as successful. The user's credits go up.

The e2e tests cover the same flow, plus a short payment that grants nothing. With the app running, run them from `e2e-tests`:

```sh
E2E_PAYMENT_PROCESSOR=mpt npx playwright test tests/mptPaymentTests.spec.ts
```

### Revenue in the admin dashboard

The daily stats job reads total revenue from MPT's `GET /api/intents/stats`: the money received for paid intents, plus late payments, since those grant credits too. Underpaid money is not counted.

### Deploying

When you deploy, set the production `MPT_SECRET_KEY`, `MPT_WEBHOOK_SECRET` and `MPT_TARGET_ADDRESS` on your server, and register your production webhook URL with MPT.
